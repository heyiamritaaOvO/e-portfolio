Skill 04 of 04

Initiative

Owning the quality of the outcome, beyond the task I was given.

Context

During my work as an Engagement Officer at Union NSW, my team was responsible for collecting survey responses from food delivery riders as part of a project on rider safety and working conditions. We aimed to collect 200 responses within two weeks. After collecting around 130, our manager informed us that the data we had gathered did not meet the project requirements, and the team needed to reconsider how we were collecting it.

Evidence of development

Our manager told us the data did not meet the project’s requirements: the questionnaire was fixed and long, so riders short on time often skipped the most important questions. I proposed asking the five most essential questions first in a brief conversation, so those who could not finish still gave the core information.

The team refined the idea before we used it; we collected nearly 400 responses in the end, and I personally collected over 120, which is the highest in the team.

Critical reflection

Parker et al. (2006) describe proactive behaviour as action that changes a situation rather than adapts to it: gathering more riders would have been adapting, while changing the method was the proactive move that took me beyond the task I was given. The questionnaire had been finalised by others and my role was only to collect responses, but I treated the quality of the data as my responsibility. This is what Parker et al. call a flexible role orientation: owning problems beyond one’s immediate tasks.

They also note that proactive behaviour carries risk, as my proposal was untested, and cutting to five questions risked losing context, which the team’s input resolved. In future I would flag a problem with a method as soon as I noticed it, rather than waiting until the results fell short.

Literature: Parker, S. K., Williams, H. M., & Turner, N. (2006). Modeling the antecedents of proactive behavior at work. Journal of Applied Psychology, 91(3), 636–652. https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.91.3.636
