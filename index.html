About Me

Hi! I’m Rita.

I’m a Master of Logistics and Supply Chain Management student in USYD, with hands-on Australian experience across airline catering, community engagement and frontline retail. I approach problems with evidence rather than assumption, bring a team’s separate contributions together into one coherent result, and work from what a client actually needs rather than only what they ask for.

Beyond that, I look for a better way of doing things, not just completing the task in front of me. I’m seeking a graduate role in supply chain, operations or procurement, where attention to detail, bilingual communication in English and Mandarin, and a genuine willingness to learn add value from the start.
